How It Works

Multi-Modal AI Architecture

MACS-Net combines clinical features with spatial attention mechanisms to deliver a holistic stroke risk assessment.

Clinical Feature Encoder

Processes 11 clinical indicators including age, hypertension, glucose, BMI, and lifestyle factors through a dense attention network.

Spatial Attention Module

Identifies critical brain regions with potential lesion activity by applying attention masks to neuroimaging spatial feature maps.

Cross-Modal Fusion

Attention-driven fusion layer aligns clinical risk factors with spatial brain patterns to produce the final risk probability.

Personalised Roadmap

Generates evidence-based, step-by-step prevention and recovery plans tailored to each patient's unique clinical profile.
